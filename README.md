# Public-Interest Infrastructure Research

Independent research on public infrastructure, consumer protection, and off-grid energy, with open tools for other researchers and consumer advocates.

## Projects

1. **Coastal EV charging access** – Using California Public Records Act requests and data analysis to study public EV charger access and pricing on public coastal land in San Diego.
2. **Off-grid power analysis** – Logging and modeling real-world energy use and storage capacity in mobile/off-grid living.
3. **Commodity marketplace fraud** – Comparing bulk copper and scrap-metal offers on B2B marketplaces with LME benchmark prices to flag likely scams.

## Planned tools

- Parsers to organize public-records responses
- Data pipelines and dashboards for charger pricing and energy logs
- A lightweight classifier that flags scam commodity listings

## Status

Early stage. Tools and findings will be published here as open source.
